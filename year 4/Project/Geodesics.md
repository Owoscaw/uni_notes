
# Parallel transport:

With all our machinery around the [[Covariant derivative|covariant derivative]], it is not clear what the covariant derivative is measuring change with respect to. It turns out that it measures the rate of change of tensors, the covariant derivative quantifies the instantaneous rate of change of a tensor field in comparison to what the tensor field would be if it were "parallel transported":![[Geodesics 2026-11-10 15.04.37.excalidraw]]
