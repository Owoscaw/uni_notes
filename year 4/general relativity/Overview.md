
General relativity is a classical theory of gravity, based on the notion of gravity being an expression of the curvature of spacetime. Einstein explored the feeling of "fictitious forces" in an accelerated reference frame, the feeling of being pushed back in your car seat when you floor it. The only force in that scenario would be the force of the seat pushing your body forward, Einstein postulates this is what we experience constantly and the "fictitious force" is gravity itself. This is the Principle of Equivalence: The effects of a gravitational field are locally indistinguishable from being in an accelerated reference frame.

Newtonian theory dictates $\underline{F}=m\underline{a}$ for our inertial frame problem, as well as $\underline{F}=m\underline{g}$ for gravitational force. The fact that the mass in each equation is identical under Newtonian theory is a "weird coincidence".
